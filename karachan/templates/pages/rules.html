Site Rules

Attempting to find loopholes around these rules will result in a permanent ban.

  1. Adult Content
    NSFW posts and adult material must be spoilered (unless board description says NSFW). Moderators reserve the right to spoiler and delete your files if deemed appropriate. This site is strictly 18+ and minors must discontinue accessing it. If you're found to be a minor you will be banned.
  2. No Illegal Content
    This is a site that tries to permit most forms of speech, but we do not allow posting, sharing, or requesting illegal material. That includes copyrighted content, anything depicting minors/underaged individuals (e.g. lolicon/shotacon - this is a touchy topic we can't comfortably handle with our hosting providers, so go somewhere else), child exploitation material, animal crush/abuse, or anything else violating applicable laws. Incidents regarding child exploitation material will be reported to relevant authorities.
  3. No Spam
    Repetitive posts, flooding, advertising irrelevant sites or platforms that don't pertain to the site, or deliberately disrupting discussions is not allowed.
  4. Stay on Topic
    Post content relevant to the specific board or thread you're contributing to. Try to maintain decent post quality, avoid low-effort posts unless explicitly permitted by the board. We will not ban you for this unless you're really making it hard to read a thread.
  5. No Personal Information
    Do not post or request personal identifying information of yourself or others, such as real names, addresses, phone numbers, or personal social media accounts.
  6. Report Violations
    Use the report function responsibly. Abuse of the reporting feature will lead to a ban.

Information Stored by the Site

For transparency, here's information the site collects when you interact with it:

  • IP Addresses:
    stored temporarily for moderation purposes and to prevent abuse (spam, harassment, ban evasion).
  • Posts and Content:
    user-generated content like text, images, and media uploaded to the site.
  • Cookies:
    used to enhance your experience (e.g., keeping your session alive and saving preferences).
  • Browser Information:
    we may collect limited browser/device data for basic fingerprinting to help detect abuse and improve site security.

Legal Procedure

In the event of a legal request from law enforcement (such as a subpoena, warrant, or valid court order), we will comply as required by law. If possible and not prohibited by the legal request, we may attempt to notify affected users before disclosing any data.
